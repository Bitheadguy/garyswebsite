WHY 1131?

A name rooted in Hebrews 11:31.

The name 1131 PRESS comes from Hebrews 11:31. It is a reminder that faith can transform a life and that God often works through unexpected people and unexpected circumstances.

While each project is different, every book published under 1131 PRESS shares the same purpose: to encourage readers to discover what may be hidden beneath the surface.
