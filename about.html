How does an engineer end up writing thrillers, drawing worms, and exploring overlooked truths in Scripture?

ENGINEER. AUTHOR. CARTOONIST.

Different projects. The same habit of looking closer.

Warren G. Harper is a licensed professional engineer, novelist, and cartoonist from Alabama. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from the University of Alabama in Huntsville.

His professional career includes extensive experience in electrical engineering, detention electronics, and controls.

His creative work ranges from the near-future speculative thriller The Code Redeemer to the hand-drawn humor of Wormy & Friends and the developing devotional I Never Noticed That Before!

The projects look very different, but they share one creative instinct: discovering what may be hidden beneath the surface.

01

Stories

Technology, freedom, conspiracy, faith, and the choices people make when systems demand obedience.

02

Humor

Simple drawings that find absurdity in work, relationships, church, sports, and ordinary life.

03

Insights

Brief discoveries from Scripture shared as a fellow reader, not as someone claiming to have noticed everything first.
