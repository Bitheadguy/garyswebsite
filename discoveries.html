DIG DEEPER

A book for readers who want to dig deeper into familiar Scripture.

Have you ever read a familiar passage of Scripture and suddenly noticed something you had never seen before?

Over the years, I’ve found myself making discoveries in Scripture that made me stop, reread a passage, and think, “I never noticed that before.”

Some were simple observations. Others changed the way I understood passages I had read for years. Those discoveries inspired me to begin writing this book.

This book simply shares some of the personal discoveries I’ve made while studying God’s Word. My hope is that it will encourage you to make discoveries of your own.
